HOUSING SETTINGS

"CONFIGURATION"


House Settings is a menu that controls global settings for a specific house.
Housing Settings
Contents Description
Time Selector Set the time and whether the daylight cycle is enabled or disabled. (Default: 6 am, Daylight cycle disabled)
Max Players Set the maximum number of players allowed in the house at one time, up to 150. The "Dynamic" option changes the cap depending on server resources and performance (max 250). (Default: 30)
House Name Set the name of the house displayed in the My Houses menu and the House Browser. (Default: " House")
Plot Size Change the maximum amount of buildable space; upgradeable with Mystery Dust. Sizes: 31x31, 41x41, 51x51, 101x101x101, 255x255x255 (Housing+ only). Sizes of 101x101x101 and above work with any theme, though some themes may cut off at these sizes. Changing the theme resets everything outside the original 51x51 area.
House Tags Apply categories to the house to make it easier to find its given content. Up to three are selectable at once.
House Language Apply a language tag to the house to make foreign language houses more discoverable.
Cookie Jar View how many cookies the house has and how many cookie packs the player has left to give out for the week.
Toggles Toggle global features like fly mode, fall damage, hunger, block breaking, and weather.
Player Data Controls what is saved about players after they leave the house. Player states and player variables can each be kept for a set duration, up to 30 days, or up to 1 year with Housing+, and individual variables can have their own duration. The saved state types can also be toggled: location and flight status; parkour progress; health, max health, and food level; inventory and armor; Ender Chest contents; held item slot; active potion effects; and team. (Default: Durations disabled)
PvP + Damage Settings Configure player vs. player combat, damage rules, knockback, and respawn behavior.
Fishing Settings Customize fishing catch rates, custom loot tables, and fishing rules.
Reset House Completely clear all built blocks, themes, and settings back to a default empty plot.
