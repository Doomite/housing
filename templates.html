What is a Template?

Templates are pre-designed scripts made using a scratch-like editor or HTSL (Housing Text Scripting Language) that can be easily imported into player houses using mods. They provide a quick and convenient way to add functionality to your house without having to create everything from scratch. Clicking on a template will send you to the website Housing Studio, as it contains more detailed information and allows for importing, creating, and sharing templates.
