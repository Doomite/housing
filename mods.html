What do Housing Mods do?

Housing mods are Minecraft modifications to the Hypixel Housing game that add new features, improve existing functionality, or provide additional tools for players to enhance their housing experience. Mainly, they focus on automating repetitive tasks and improving the overall gameplay mechanics, such as importing code.
