House Settings is a menu that controls global settings for a specific house.
Housing Settings
Contents Description
Time Selector Select the time of your house: noon, midnight, day. Can also be set with /time
Max Players Set the maximum number of players allowed in your house at once.
House Name Change the title of your house displayed on the house browser and scoreboard.
Plot Size Upgrade your house plot dimensions (31x31, 41x41, 51x51) using Mystery Dust.
House Tags Assign categories/tags to your house to help players find it in the public browser.
House Language Specify the primary spoken language of your housing plot.
Cookie Jar View, manage, or clear the cookies given to your house by visiting players.
Toggles Toggle global features like fly mode, fall damage, hunger, block breaking, and weather.
Player Data View, edit, or reset saved stats, inventories, and custom variables for players.
PvP + Damage Settings Configure player vs. player combat, damage rules, knockback, and respawn behavior.
Fishing Settings Customize fishing catch rates, custom loot tables, and fishing rules.
Reset House Completely clear all built blocks, themes, and settings back to a default empty plot.
